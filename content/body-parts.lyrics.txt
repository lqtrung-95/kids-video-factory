Touch your head and touch your nose,
wiggle, wiggle all your toes!
Two little eyes to see the sky,
two little ears hear birds fly by!
Head and hands and knees and feet,
move your body to the beat!
Clap your hands, clap, clap, clap,
pat your knees, pat, pat, pat!
Stomp your feet upon the ground,
shake your body all around!
Head and hands and knees and feet,
move your body to the beat!
