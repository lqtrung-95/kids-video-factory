Mommy, Daddy, sister, me,
we're a happy family!
Brother likes to run and play,
baby giggles all the day!
Family, family, love you so,
everywhere together we go!
Grandma bakes a cake for me,
Grandpa reads beneath a tree!
Hugs and kisses, big and small,
I love my family most of all!
Family, family, love you so,
everywhere together we go!
