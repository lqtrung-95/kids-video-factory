Wheels go round on the busy street,
beep, beep, beep, now tap your feet!
The fire truck goes wee-oo, wee-oo,
rushing out to help me and you!
Beep and vroom and choo-choo-choo,
so many sounds for me and you!
The train goes chugging down the track,
choo-choo forward, choo-choo back!
The big boat sails across the sea,
toot, toot, toot, come sail with me!
Beep and vroom and choo-choo-choo,
so many sounds for me and you!
