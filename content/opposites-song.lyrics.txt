Big and small and up and down,
opposites are all around!
A mouse is small, an elephant's big,
a tall giraffe, a short little pig!
Opposites, opposites, let's play,
opposites are fun today!
Hot like the sun and cold like ice,
fast and slow, now that is nice!
Open the door, then close it tight,
the sun is day, the moon is night!
Opposites, opposites, let's play,
opposites are fun today!
