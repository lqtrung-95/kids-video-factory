Three, two, one, blast off with me,
count the stars that we can see!
One, two, three, the rockets fly,
four, five, six up in the sky!
Up we go to outer space,
counting stars all over the place!
Seven, eight, the planets glow,
nine and ten, and off we go!
Moons and comets shining bright,
astronauts float through the night!
Up we go to outer space,
counting stars all over the place!
