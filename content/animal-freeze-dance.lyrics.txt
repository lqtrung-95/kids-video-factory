Hop like a bunny, hop, hop, hop,
hop, hop, hop and never stop!

big, big feet go romp, romp, romp!

when I say freeze, don't make a sound!

wiggle, wiggle all around!

flap your wings and fly, fly, fly!

when I say freeze, don't make a sound!
