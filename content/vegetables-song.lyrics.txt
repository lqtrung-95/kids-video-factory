Crunchy carrots, orange and long,
veggies make us big and strong!
Green broccoli, like little trees,
and tiny round and yummy peas!
Eat your veggies every day,
they help us grow and run and play!
Red tomatoes, juicy and sweet,
yellow corn is fun to eat!
Potatoes, pumpkins, peppers too,
so many veggies, good for you!
Eat your veggies every day,
they help us grow and run and play!
