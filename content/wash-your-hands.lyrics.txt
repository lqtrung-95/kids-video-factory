Germs, germs, go away,
wash your hands every day!
Turn the water, get them wet,
soap and bubbles, don't forget!
Scrub, scrub, rub them clean,
the cleanest hands you've ever seen!
Front and back and in between,
fingers, thumbs, now squeaky clean!
Rinse it off, splash, splash, splash,
dry your hands, quick as a flash!
Scrub, scrub, rub them clean,
the cleanest hands you've ever seen!
