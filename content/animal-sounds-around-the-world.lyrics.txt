Listen, listen, hear that sound?
Animals sing the whole world round!
The puppy barks, the kitty sings,
the duck goes splashing, flap your wings!
Woof and meow, quack and crow,
every country says it so!
Little duck is swimming far,
paddle, paddle, there you are!
Sun comes up, the rooster wakes,
what a big, big noise he makes!
Woof and meow, quack and crow,
every country says it so!
Let's fly around the world today,
and hear what all the animals say!
