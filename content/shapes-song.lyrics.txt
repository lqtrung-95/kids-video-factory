Circle, square and triangle too,
shapes are everywhere for you!
The sun is round, a circle bright,
a star is twinkling in the night!
Shapes, shapes, look around,
so many shapes are to be found!
A box is square with corners four,
a rectangle is like a door!
A slice of pizza, three sides, see?
A heart for you and one for me!
Shapes, shapes, look around,
so many shapes are to be found!
