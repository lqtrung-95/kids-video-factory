Twelve months make a year go round,
let's all sing them, what a sound!
January, February, March,
April, May, the flowers start!
Twelve months in every year,
sing them all and give a cheer!
June and July, the summer sun,
August, September, so much fun!
October, November, falling leaves,
December, snow is on the trees!
Twelve months in every year,
sing them all and give a cheer!
