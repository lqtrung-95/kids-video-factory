Look up high, what do you see?
A rainbow smiling down at me!
Red like apples, orange like the sun,
yellow bananas, yum, yum, yum!
Colors, colors, shining bright,
a rainbow full of color and light!
Green like frogs that jump and play,
blue like the sea on a sunny day!
Purple grapes and pink balloons,
let's all sing a color tune!
Colors, colors, shining bright,
a rainbow full of color and light!
