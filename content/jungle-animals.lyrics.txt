Deep in the jungle, what do I hear?
Animal voices far and near!
The lion roars, the monkey calls,
the parrot chatters, the water falls!
Roar and squeak and hiss, hiss, hiss,
jungle sounds you cannot miss!
The elephant trumpets loud and clear,
the tiger growls, he's very near!
The zebra runs, the giraffe is tall,
we love the jungle animals all!
Roar and squeak and hiss, hiss, hiss,
jungle sounds you cannot miss!
